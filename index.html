Every class,
same arc

An hour that builds from the ground up. Beginners do the first version of each part; you get harder options as you progress.

  1. 1

    Warm up

    Joints, wrists and shoulders first. Every class.

  2. 2

    Toprock

    The standing steps that open every round. Groove, timing and style.

  3. 3

    Footwork

    Moving on the floor with control: the six step, threads and your own variations.

  4. 4

    Freezes

    Holding shapes like the baby freeze and chair freeze, built up step by step.

  5. 5

    Power basics

    An intro to spins and rotations, only with proper progressions and mats.

  6. 6

    Cypher

    Music on, circle up. Hearing the breaks, battle etiquette, respect, and where hip hop comes from.
